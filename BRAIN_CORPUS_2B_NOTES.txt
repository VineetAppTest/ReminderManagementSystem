Sprint Brain-Corpus-2B: Corpus Regression Calibration + Single-Turn Parser Hardening

Purpose
- Reduce false corpus failures caused by strict phrase-vs-normalized-output comparisons.
- Harden single-turn reminder parsing where event details and reminder instructions appear in the same sentence.

Fixes included
1. Corpus regression calibration
   - Morning/evening/night corpus expectations are normalized to the app's concrete times.
   - Time text such as "10 am" is normalized to "10:00 am" for comparison.
   - Weekday/next-weekday labels such as Monday, Friday, next Tuesday are no longer treated as hard phrase equality failures when the app correctly resolves them to concrete dates.
   - Today/tomorrow remain hard assertions.

2. Single-turn parser hardening
   - Direct commands such as "Remind me to call Raj tomorrow at 8 pm" now allow the event/reminder time to be extracted from inside the reminder command segment.
   - Pure missing-task forms such as "remind me at 4 pm" still remain blocked and ask what the reminder is about.
   - Single-sentence event + reminder cases now preserve event date before parsing reminder alerts.
   - Before-event single-sentence cases such as "Team meeting at 5 pm today, remind me half an hour before" can calculate the offset from the event time.
   - Multiple-alert single-sentence cases such as "Meeting at 8 pm tomorrow, remind me today at 7 and tomorrow at 6" are handled more safely.

- src/brain/miniViktorCorpusRegression.ts
