RemindIQ Sprint Alarm-3A: Repeating Alarm Visibility + Full-Screen Contrast Fix

What changed
1. Repeating alarms remain visible in the Reminders section instead of being archived after their first trigger.
2. Repeating alarms show a visible "Repeating" chip and a "Next alarm" line.
3. Added a "Stop repeat" action on repeating alarm cards.
4. Stop repeat cancels the native Android alarm and marks the reminder done.
5. In-app repeat fallback now advances the next due time for hourly, daily, and weekly repeats while the app is open.
6. Android full-screen alarm screen now uses stronger contrast and shows body text separately.
7. Android alarm notification no longer uses colorized mode, reducing the risk of unreadable same-color text.

Validation performed
- npm run build: passed
- npx cap sync android: passed

Mandatory tester checks
1. Create a repeating alarm, e.g. "Set alarm every 1 hour" or "Repeat alarm every 30 minutes starting now".
2. Confirm it appears in Reminders with a Repeating chip.
3. Confirm Stop repeat is visible.
4. Tap Stop repeat and confirm it moves to Done and stops native scheduling.
5. Trigger a full-screen alarm and confirm text is readable.
