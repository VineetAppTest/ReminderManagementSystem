4. Repeating alarm task extraction
   - Set a daily alarm for medicine at 9 am -> task: medicine.

5. Voice shortcut hardening
   - meeting with Raj at ATM -> ATM is treated as 8 pm.
   - meeting with Rajat ATM already continues through existing normalization.
