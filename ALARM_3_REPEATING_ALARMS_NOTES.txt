Sprint Alarm-3: Repeating Alarms

This build adds first-pass support for recurring alarm commands:

1. "Create a repetitive alarm with 1 hour interval"
   - Interprets as repeat every 1 hour.
   - Asks when the first alarm should start.

2. "Create a repetitive alarm after 1 hour"
   - Interprets as first alarm 1 hour from now and repeats every 1 hour.

3. "Create a repetitive alarm daily at 2 pm"
   - Interprets as daily repeating alarm at 2:00 PM.

4. "Weekly repeat alarm at 5 pm Monday"
   - Interprets as weekly repeating alarm on Monday at 5:00 PM.

5. "Create a daily or weekly repeat alarm"
   - Asks the user to choose daily or weekly and provide the time.

Native Android behaviour:
- Repeat metadata is passed into the native alarm bridge.
- The AlarmReceiver schedules the next occurrence after each firing.
- One-time alarm logic remains unchanged.

Hard limitation:
- This is not yet a full recurring-alarm management module. Editing/cancelling an entire series is basic and should be tested carefully.
- Alarm-3 should be treated as UAT build, not production-stable.
