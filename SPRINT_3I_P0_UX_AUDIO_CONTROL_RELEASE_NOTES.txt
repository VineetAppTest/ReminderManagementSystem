Included fixes:
1. Full-screen alarm readability and premium UI hardening
   - High-contrast full-screen alarm layout.
   - Long alarm titles are contained inside a scroll-safe title panel.
   - No horizontal overflow for long phrases.
   - Stronger background contrast and clearer time/title hierarchy.

2. Full-screen Snooze / Done / Stop Repeat controls
   - Snooze 5 min, Done, and Stop repeat are directly visible on the full-screen alarm.
   - Done dismisses the current full-screen alarm and stops MiniViktor/audio cleanup.
   - Snooze creates a fresh one-time snoozed alarm 5 minutes later.
   - Stop repeat disables future repeat occurrences and marks the repeat as done.

3. Audio cleanup hardening
   - MiniViktor speech and in-app alarm tone are stopped on Done, Snooze, Stop repeat, mark done, and stop repeat.
   - Added cleanup on pagehide, beforeunload, and document visibilitychange.
   - Added vibration stop during cleanup.

4. Victor-like MiniViktor voice tuning
   - Lower, calmer, slower voice settings.
   - Selects best available en-IN / en-GB / en-US device voice where available.
   - Falls back safely when device/browser voice list is limited.

5. Compact reminder/alarm cards
   - Reminder cards now use dynamic height.
   - Raw text is hidden behind a Details expander.
   - Repeat labels are compact.
   - Mobile card action layout is more compact.
