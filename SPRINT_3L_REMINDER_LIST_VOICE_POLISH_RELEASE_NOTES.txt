RemindIQ Sprint Alarm-3L — Reminder List + Voice Polish
Build: RemindIQ-Sprint-Alarm-3L-Reminder-List-Voice-Polish
Base: RemindIQ-Sprint-Alarm-3L-MiniViktor-Context-UX-Closeout

Included in this polish build:
1. Reminder list cards are now more compact and dynamic in height.
2. Long reminder/alarm phrases are clamped/wrapped safely instead of stretching the card or screen.
3. Reminder action buttons are smaller, pill-shaped, and better suited for mobile.
4. Repeating alarm labels remain visible, but raw long input is tucked under Details.
5. MiniViktor spoken replies are shorter and less robotic.
6. Voice selection now prefers higher-quality English India / natural / male-style voices where the device/browser exposes them.
7. MiniViktor pitch and rate were lowered slightly to make the voice less sharp.

Important limitation:
The exact MiniViktor voice depends on what the Android WebView/browser/device exposes. This build improves selection and speech shaping, but it cannot force a custom Victor voice unless we later add a recorded/custom TTS voice pipeline.

Local validation commands:
npm.cmd install
npm.cmd run build
npx.cmd cap sync android
npx.cmd cap open android

Suggested checks:
1. Create several long-title reminders and confirm the reminder list stays compact.
2. Create repeating alarms and confirm Stop repeat is still visible.
3. Turn spoken replies on and confirm MiniViktor replies are shorter and less sharp.
4. Trigger a full-screen alarm and confirm 3L UX controls still work.
