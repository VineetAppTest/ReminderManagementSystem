Sprint Alarm-2B Fix 1: Native Ringing Foreground Service

Why this fix exists:
- Android may block direct background Activity launch from BroadcastReceiver.
- The previous receiver attempted to open AlarmActivity directly, so the ringing screen could fail to appear.
- This fix starts a foreground ringing service from the alarm receiver.

What changed:
1. Added RemindIqRingingService.java.
2. AlarmReceiver now starts the foreground ringing service.
3. The foreground service plays looping alarm audio and vibration.
4. The service posts a high-priority full-screen alarm notification.
5. AlarmActivity now controls the service with Stop/Done and Snooze 10 min.
6. Manifest includes foreground service permissions.

Testing:
- Uninstall old RemindIQ before installing.
- Create a reminder 2 minutes from now.
- Lock the phone.
- Expected: alarm sound/vibration + alarm notification/full-screen screen.

If full-screen screen still does not open while phone is unlocked:
- Android may show a high-priority heads-up notification instead.
- Lock-screen full-screen behaviour is the more important test.
